## Deep Hedging and Deep Calibration
Foundations of Deep Hedging and Deep Calibration in Incomplete Markets



### Deep Hedging

Deep Hedging (Buehler et al., 2019) extends the classical hedging framework by using neural networks to approximate the value function and the hedging strategy in incomplete markets.

### Deep Calibration

Deep Calibration (Han et al., 2018; Han & Keller, 2020) extends the classical calibration problem by using neural networks to fit complex models to market data.
